How it works

A fantasy world on top of real work

A Vibe Called Quest reads what your Claude Code sessions are doing from files on your Mac: transcripts, git and project docs. It turns that into a world. Every line reads like a standalone game, and the real fact sits underneath in grey.

Guide

Start playing

You need macOS, Node.js and Claude Code. Ollama is optional; it writes the story. Without it, Quest uses built-in lines.

  1. Get the game

    git clone https://github.com/thenewurbankid-web/claude_quest.git ~/Repositories/claude-quest
cd ~/Repositories/claude-quest && npm install

  2. Make your config

    cp config.example.json config.json

    It holds the server, story-model and scan settings. Your lands are charted in game, not here.

  3. Connect it to Claude Code (once)

    npm run install-hook

    This adds Quest's hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json and keeps a backup first. The hooks deliver your answers to a working session and make STOP work.

  4. Start the world

    npm start

    Open localhost:4777. Choose New Game and Warden Ash will meet you. Your first land is the game itself.

  5. Chart your projects

    Talk to the Cartographer in the hub and pick a repo. It appears as a new town on the map. You can rename it, recolour it and give it notes in the Map Room (/settings).

Your first riddle

When a Claude asks you something, a Sphinx appears in its town and the courier brings word. Walk up, press Z and choose an answer or write your own. Quest never answers for you. Your answer is sent to Claude as the original option text, not the story version.

Docs

Reference

Three ways to play

  • Local: npm start on your Mac, then play at localhost:4777. Everything is live.
  • Linked: a hosted copy of the game paired with your Mac over an end-to-end encrypted link. Turn it on with CQ_LINK=1 npm start, then open the pairing link it prints. The hosted page isn't published yet.
  • Browser: nothing linked. You play on your last saved world in this browser. Actions wait until a link exists.

The Map Room (/settings)

  • Each land's name, colour and notes. The storyteller reads your notes.
  • Sound, music, autoplay delay, subtext on or off
  • Minimap position and size
  • Pairing, saves, export and import, checkpoints

The Guild Hall

  • Post a quest, send word, call off a quest, send the guild home
  • Runs on Paperclip at 127.0.0.1:3100 when it's up
  • When it's down, the hall shows "closed" and the rest of the world still plays

Where your data lives

  • data/: your save, inbox and events. Git-ignored. Never reset it, because the inbox holds your real answers.
  • config.json: local settings, git-ignored
  • areas/<id>.md: your notes for each land

What costs tokens

The world itself costs nothing. It's built from files and narrated by a local model. Claude tokens are spent only on work you send: delivering answers to a session that has stopped, posting a Guild quest, or sending word to an agent. The game asks before each of these.

House rules

  • Quest never answers a question on your behalf, even on autoplay.
  • Your Mac never accepts incoming connections. The link only connects outward.
  • What leaves the Mac in linked mode is what the game shows, without document contents or run prompts.
  • The storyteller turns facts into lore. It never invents progress.
